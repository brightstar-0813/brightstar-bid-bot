1. Healthcare
Patient 360 + EHR Integration Platform

Verified public inspiration: Damco worked on EHR modernization for a hospital, including legacy EHR data migration to Cerner, CRM integration, analytics, eight developers, three QA engineers, two data-conversion specialists, and a project manager.

Enterprise blueprint:

Business problem:
Patient information is fragmented between EHR, CRM, scheduling, contact-center, and analytics systems.

Salesforce stack:
Health Cloud, Service Cloud, OmniStudio, Experience Cloud, MuleSoft, CRM Analytics.

Core model:
Person Account, CarePlan, CareTeam, ClinicalEncounter, Case, ContactPointAddress, Provider, custom Referral and Appointment objects.

Integration architecture:
Epic/Cerner → FHIR R4 → MuleSoft → Salesforce Health Cloud.

Use:
REST/FHIR APIs, Named Credentials, OAuth/JWT, Platform Events, Change Data Capture.

Automation:
Flows for intake and routing.
Apex for complex synchronization.
OmniScripts for guided patient registration.
Integration Procedures for FHIR orchestration.

Security:
OWD private, permission-set groups, field-level protection for PHI, audit history, encryption strategy.

Scale scenario:
Millions of patient records, high-volume clinical updates, asynchronous reconciliation jobs.

Interview challenge:
Prevent duplicate patients when EHR and Salesforce have different identifiers.

Solution:
MRN + external system ID matching, probabilistic duplicate detection, matching rules, integration idempotency keys and reconciliation queues.

2. Healthcare
Epic Patient Search and Registration

This one has direct Salesforce technical evidence.

Salesforce's archived accelerator combines Health Cloud, OmniStudio, Person Accounts, Epic FHIR R4, JWT authentication and optional MuleSoft integration.

Architecture:

User searches by:
MRN, FHIR ID, DOB, name, phone, demographics.

Flow:

Health Cloud
→ OmniScript
→ Integration Procedure
→ JWT / Named Credential
→ MuleSoft or direct API
→ Epic FHIR Patient endpoint

If patient exists:
Map Epic response to Person Account.

If patient does not exist:
Create patient through FHIR Patient.Create and associate the resulting identifier with Salesforce.

Technical components:
OmniScript, FlexCard, DataRaptors, Integration Procedures, Apex JWT helper, Person Accounts.

This is excellent for Senior Developer, Health Cloud Consultant and Solution Architect interviews.
